### Procedure

<div style="float: left; width:100%;">

#### 1. Accurately mark the locations where the base plate is to be placed at the site.<br>
</div>

<div style="float: left; width:100%;">
<center>
  <img src="images/proced1.png" height="220px"><br>
  Fig.2 Base Plates
</center>
</div>

<div style="float: left; width:100%;">
  
#### 2. Bring all the relevant materials and apparatus required to erect formwork.<br>
</div>

<div style="float: left; width:100%;">
<center>
  <img src="images/proced2.png" height="220px"><br>
  Fig.3 CT props with U-Head
</center>
</div>

<div style="float: left; width:100%;">

#### 3. For each beam, place 4 base plates. E.g., if two beams are cast, 8 base plates will be placed.<br>
</div>

<div style="float: left; width:100%;">
  
#### 4. On the base plates, place CT props<br>
</div>

<div style="float: left; width:100%;">
  
#### 5. Mount U-head on the top of the CT prop<br>
</div>

<div style="float: left; width:100%;">
<center>
  <img src="images/proced3.png" height="220px"><br>
  Fig.4 HD Tower with bracings
</center>
</div>

<div style="float: left; width:100%;">
  
#### 6. Attach horizontal and diagonal  bracings to the HD tower to provide lateral  support and stiffness to the structure<br>
</div>

<div style="float: left; width:100%;">
<center>
  <img src="images/proced4.png" height="220px"><br>
  Fig.5 Aluminum and Timber beam attached
</center>
</div>

<div style="float: left; width:100%;">
  
#### 7. Attach an aluminum beam to the basic frame on both HD towers. Aluminum beams are used as they are lightweight and, hence, easy to install. Place 2 aluminum beams in the direction of the concrete beam for each HD tower.<br>
</div>

<div style="float: left; width:100%;">
  
#### 8. Place timber beams on top of the aluminum beams. The orientation of timber beams shall be perpendicular to that of aluminum beams.<br>
</div>

<div style="float: left; width:100%;">
<center>
  <img src="images/proced5.png" height="220px"><br>
  Fig.6. Sheathing with nails to attach it to timber beam
</center>
</div>

<div style="float: left; width:100%;">
  
#### 9. On top of the timber beams, place sheathing and use nails to secure the sheathing.<br>
</div>

<div style="float: left; width:100%;">
<center>
  <img src="images/proced6.png" height="220px"><br>
  Fig.6. Sheathing and Timber Beam attached to BFS
</center>
</div>

<div style="float: left; width:100%;">
  
#### 10. Attach beam forming support to the timber beams. BFS (beam forming support) helps support the beam being cast.<br>
</div>

<div style="float: left; width:100%;">
  
#### 11. Attach timber beams to the BFS firmly in the direction of the beam to be cast. These timber beams will help resist horizontal loads on the sheathing during concreting and curing.<br>
</div>

<div style="float: left; width:100%;">
<center>
  <img src="images/proced7.png" height="220px"><br>
  Fig.7 Timber Beam on top of Aluminum Beam with Short Prop as support
</center>
</div>

<div style="float: left; width:100%;">
  
#### 12.  Attach short props for slab formwork<br>
</div>

<div style="float: left; width:100%;">
  
#### 13.  To these short props, attach U-heads to make a place for the aluminum base plates to be placed<br>
</div>

<div style="float: left; width:100%;">
  
#### 14.  On the tip of aluminum beams, place timber beams in a perpendicular direction.<br>
</div>

<div style="float: left; width:100%;">
<center>
  <img src="images/proced8.png" height="220px"><br>
  Fig.8 Placement of Sheathing
</center>
</div>

<div style="float: left; width:100%;">
  
#### 15. On the top of timber beams, place sheathing and sure it to timber beams using nails.<br>
</div>
